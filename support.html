Care

The quiet part.

Shipping, returns, and a way to write to us.

Shipping

Orders ship within the United States in 3–6 business days. Complimentary shipping starts at $75. Under that, shipping is $6. Checkout shows the final number.

Returns

30 days. Unused, in the box it arrived in. The buyer pays return shipping unless the object arrives damaged or not as described. The return address is printed on the confirmation once a returns location is connected. Refunds go to the original payment method after the return is checked in.

Privacy

Orders and checkout are handled by Shopify. An email is used for the order and, if you join the list, for an occasional note about a new object. We do not sell personal information.

Terms

Colors on a screen and colors in a room are close, not identical. Measurements are added only from a checked sample. The contract of sale is the one Shopify Checkout presents. These pages are the storefront copy, not a substitute for that checkout.
